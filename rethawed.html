WHAT IS RETHAWED?
Breathe new life into Tony Hawk's American Wasteland with reTHAWed, a comprehensive
mod that injects the game with a massive dose of fresh content and long-awaited fixes.
Engine Enhancements
Enjoy a true modern experience with widescreen support, flexible controller handling, and other fixes. reTHAWed brings THAW's dated PC port to the new age.
Loads of Levels
Play through nearly every level from Tony Hawk's Pro Skater to Tony Hawk's Project 8. Re-experience new and old maps with all that reTHAWed has to offer.
New Music System
Completely rewritten and properly randomized soundtrack system! Install multiple playlists at once and shuffle between them with ease. Toggle individual songs or even entire playlists.
New Mechanics and Tech
Skate your way. Enable old-school mechanics like Big Drops, or new-gen tricks like the Footplant. Craft combos to your liking with a flexible trick system and a plethora of options.
Attention to Detail
Enjoy the American Wasteland in new high-resolution fidelity with remade graphics, enhanced fonts, and more. reTHAWed uses the best versions of assets and source material when possible.
Achievements and Rewards
Track your adventure with milestones, achievements, and rewards. Earn heaps of new content and accomplishments, whether skating with other pros or shredding it solo.
Mod Support
Mix and match characters, items, and more with full support for user-created mods. Install custom levels or play with the skater of your dreams with expandable content.
Intact Story Mode
Experience the full storyline of the original game and live through your custom skater's Los Angeles adventure. Build the Skate Ranch piece-by-piece and meet other skaters along the way.
Massive Character Roster
Dive deep into nostalgia and play with nearly any character of your dreams. Enjoy hundreds of characters ported from other games in the series, obscure and well-known, including reTHAWed originals.
Cutscenes and Events
Trigger level events, cause destruction, and check out new cutscenes in full fidelity. Ported maps from other entries make a return with cinematics, secrets, and so much more.
Functional Vehicles
Bust a trick with Jesse James' segway. Fire up the Australian mini-kart. Online or offline, land extreme combos on insane vehicles from Tony Hawk's Underground 2 and more.
New Classic Modes
Blast to the past with Classic Mode making a fully functional return. Go above and beyond with other tours from Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3 and Tony Hawk's Project 8.
Revived Multiplayer
Take advantage of OpenSpy's servers to meet and skate with players from around the world. Compete in gamemodes, earn achievements, or simply hang out in free skate.
Just a Tribute
Experience what made Neversoft one of a kind with the inclusion of assets across the company's game library. Re-live an era with content from Apocalypse, Guitar Hero, GUN, and more.
And so much more. With frequent updates that consistently add content, reTHAWed
is the ultimate way to revisit and revitalize Tony Hawk's American Wasteland.
